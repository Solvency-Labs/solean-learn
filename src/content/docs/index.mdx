## The whole project, in one diagram

Our job is to **formally verify `ForsVerifier.sol`** — the contract in the middle below. Everything else is either the *context* it lives in (the wallet on top) or the *tools* we prove it with (the bottom). Click any layer to jump into the track that teaches it.

<StackMap />

If that diagram looks like alphabet soup right now, that's the point of this site. By the end you'll be able to read every layer — and explain why keccak is the one box we *don't* prove.

## How to use this guide

You don't have to read top to bottom. The honest dependency order is **T1 → T2 → T3** for the *domain* and **T4 → T5** for the *tooling*; they merge in **T6**. New to everything? Follow [the learning path](/solean-learn/orientation/path/). Just need a word defined? Jump to the [glossary](/solean-learn/reference/glossary/).
